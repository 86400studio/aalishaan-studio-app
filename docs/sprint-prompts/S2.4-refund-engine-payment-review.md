Missing lookup results, a timeout or an ambiguous error never release money or permit a new submission. Provider/dashboard/automatic refunds must reduce available funds or block further compensation until reconciled. [Refund webhooks](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/refunds) can include automatic refunds of uncaptured payments. Validate identity and evidence; do not create a local approved item remedy from any signed refund event.

S1.3 shipped the public shell and fifteen pages (PR #24, merged 2026-10-22), but its Preview measured Home at a Lighthouse mobile median of **38** against D-19's ≥ 90 (the PR #24 Preview record, run 5): LCP 8.1–10.1 s, total blocking time 1.1–3.1 s, about 3,162 KiB per load.

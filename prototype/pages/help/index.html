Frequently asked questions

Useful answers about your artwork, frame and what comes next.

13 answers

Artwork & print

What exactly am I buying?

A made-to-order, framed A2 archival giclée print, not an original painting. The textures and metallic effects you see in the artwork are reproduced in print.

What paper and inks do you use?

Hahnemühle Museum Etching 350 gsm paper and 12-colour pigment inks.

Size & frame

What size is A2?

Portrait prints measure 42 × 59.4 cm; landscape prints measure 59.4 × 42 cm. These are print dimensions. Outer frame dimensions will be confirmed before ordering.

Which frames can I choose?

White ₹19,000, Black ₹21,000 or Antique Gold ₹23,000. Each is ornately carved solid wood with acrylic glazing and fitted hanging hardware.

Ordering

How many artworks are available?

Explore 22 artworks, three collections and eleven art styles. Choose a frame on the artwork page, then add it to your bag.

Can I place an order here?

This is a visual preview. You can explore a sample checkout, but no order is placed and no payment is collected.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

We accept online payment only: UPI, cards, net banking and wallets through Razorpay. If you’d prefer to pay another way, message us on WhatsApp at +91 9137624394 and we’ll help.

What happens if ordering is paused?

When the studio is working through a large number of orders we may pause new orders for a short while. Leave your email or mobile number on the bag or checkout page and we’ll tell you the moment ordering reopens. Your bag will be waiting for you.

Delivery & care

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is planned across India. Enter your PIN code on any artwork page, in your bag or at checkout to see a sample serviceability check and delivery estimate. Shipping charges, final serviceable locations and production and delivery estimates will be confirmed before orders open.

How do I care for my print?

Hang away from direct sunlight and moisture. Use a soft, dry cloth on the frame; never spray cleaning products directly onto the glazing.

Support

What if my artwork arrives damaged?

Keep photographs of the artwork and packaging. Full reporting, replacement and return conditions will be confirmed before orders open.

Can I cancel or request a refund?

Eligibility, cut-offs and refund timings are still being finalised. See the policy pages for the current status.

Are gifting options available?

Gift notes, special packaging and delivery to a recipient are not yet confirmed. You can preview a gifting enquiry on the Contact page.

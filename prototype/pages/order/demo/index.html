What happens after you press buy.

  1. STEP 01

    We confirm your order

    You will receive an email with your order details.

  2. STEP 02

    We prepare your artwork

    Your selected piece is prepared for printing, framing, and finishing.

  3. STEP 03

    We frame it carefully

    Each artwork is framed by hand and checked for finish and fit.

  4. STEP 04

    We inspect everything

    We review the print, frame, and overall presentation before packing.

  5. STEP 05

    We pack it securely

    Your artwork is protected so it travels safely.

  6. STEP 06

    It arrives at your home

    Your piece is delivered ready to style and enjoy.

Every piece is made with care, so timing can vary slightly by artwork, frame, and delivery location.
